Textbook Explanation (Page 3): Pumice is formed when hot lava with trapped air bubbles cools very rapidly. These trapped pockets make it porous and light enough to float on water. It is widely used in cosmetics, body scrubbers, and lightweight construction blocks.

2 Metamorphic Rocks Textbook Reference: Page 5 check_circle Correct (+1)

Which metamorphic rock is formed from limestone, polishes to a high shine, and was used to construct the Taj Mahal?

A. Quartzite
B. Marble check
C. Gneiss
D. Shale
auto_stories
Textbook Explanation (Page 5): When limestone undergoes intense subterranean heat and pressure, it metamorphoses into marble. Marble can be polished to a sparkling lustrous surface and is famous globally as the building stone of the Taj Mahal and royal floorings.
White marble specimen (p. 5)
3 Sedimentary Rocks Textbook Reference: Page 4 check_circle Correct (+1)

The historic Red Fort in Delhi and Qutub Minar were primarily constructed from which rock?

A. Obsidian
B. Basalt
C. Red Sandstone check
D. Chalk
auto_stories
Textbook Explanation (Page 4): Sandstone is composed of sand grains cemented by silica or iron oxide minerals. Red sandstone contains iron oxide giving it its characteristic red color, making it the choice stone for monumental structures like the Red Fort.
Red sandstone sample (p. 4)

What is the hardest naturally occurring mineral substance found on Earth?

A. Diamond check
B. Talc
C. Quartz
D. Calcite
auto_stories
Textbook Explanation (Page 2): Diamond is the hardest mineral known in nature, rating 10 on the Mohs scale of hardness. Due to this extreme hardness, it is used not only in jewelry but also in industrial rock-drilling and glass-cutting tools.
Diamond mineral crystal (p. 2)
