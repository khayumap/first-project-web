3 Major Types of Rocks

Discover how rocks are formed on Earth:

  • check_circle Igneous: Cooled molten magma (Granite, Basalt, Pumice)
  • check_circle Sedimentary: Compressed layers (Sandstone, Limestone)
  • check_circle Metamorphic: Changed by heat & pressure (Marble, Slate)

Minerals & Daily Items

From jewelry to food flavoring and timekeeping:

  • check_circle Quartz: Precision in wristwatches and clocks
  • check_circle Talc: Softest mineral, used in baby powders
  • check_circle Iron & Copper: Bridges, steel girders, and wires

Fossil Fuels Energy

Treasures formed over millions of years:
